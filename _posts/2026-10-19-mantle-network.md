I am pleased to announce the official launch of **The MANTLE NETWORK Initiative**, a new collective of scientists dedicated to studying the Earth's mantle. Our website, [mantlenetwork.org](https://mantlenetwork.org), showcases mantle science and serves as a central hub for scientific resources, webinars, and the coordination of meetings and events.
